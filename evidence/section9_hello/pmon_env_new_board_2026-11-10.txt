Observation (not a conclusion): the list has no `al` (autoload) and no `append` variable, and `ethaddr` is the default ff:ff:ff:ff:ff:ff.
This is why PMON does not start Linux by itself; the guide step 9.2.10 sets these variables with `set` and writes the kernel and root file system with `devcp`, i.e. commands that change the flash/PMON environment and need the instructor's approval.
